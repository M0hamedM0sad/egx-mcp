Exit rules (apply to every pick)

  1. Scale-out at 1R — when price reaches the scale-out level, sell half and move the stop on the remainder to breakeven. Locks in the win and removes downside on the runner.
  2. Target or trail (1.5R) — sell the runner at the target, OR trail by 1×ATR on new highs to let it run. Pick whichever you'll actually execute.
  3. Stop loss — exit immediately if the stop level trades. Use a stop-loss order with the broker, not a mental stop.
  4. Time stop (conditional) — applies ONLY if scale-out never triggered. Once half is off at 1R, the runner trails freely past the time stop.
  5. Catalyst override — if a blocking catalyst (e.g., earnings) lands during the hold, exit before the print.
  6. Re-entry — if the same ticker reappears in next Sunday's W1 top-5, extend the trail rather than close-and-reopen.
